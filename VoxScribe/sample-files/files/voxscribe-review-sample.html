Codebook

  • workflow: How the participant currently completes transcription and analysis tasks.
  • manual coding: References to spreadsheet or copy-paste coding practices.
  • pain point: Friction or delays in the analysis process.
  • theme development: Grouping evidence into higher-level themes.
  • export need: Desired output formats or report contents.
  • evidence: Passages that support findings or final reports.
